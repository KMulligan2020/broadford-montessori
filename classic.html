About Us

A Little School With a Big Heart

Broadford Montessori is a small, nurturing preschool where children are free to explore, create and grow at their own pace.

We believe the early years are about so much more than getting ready for school. They are about building confidence, curiosity, and a love of learning that lasts a lifetime. Our sessions are intentionally small so that every child is truly known and every family feels at home.

We follow the Aistear framework, Ireland's early childhood curriculum, and are fully registered with Tusla. Our sessions are held in the Broadford Scout Den in Ballinteer, a well-equipped, safe and welcoming community space.

ECCE registered for the coming year. ECCE is a free, two-year preschool programme funded by the Irish government. It provides 15 hours of free childcare per week (3 hours a day, 5 days a week) for 38 weeks a year, running September to June. Children are eligible from 2 years 8 months up to 5 years 6 months. Our sessions run 9:00am to 12:00pm with no extended day. Contact us to check availability.
